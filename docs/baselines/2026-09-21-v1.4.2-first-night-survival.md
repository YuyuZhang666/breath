# v1.4.2 First-night survival hardening

## Scope

This P1 increment starts only after the v1.4.1 request-deadline fix was
merged to `master`. It preserves the bounded night forecast and search paths
and makes three small, evidence-backed changes to first-night preparation:

- missing day-one core weapons may consume the economy gold reserve;
- the default opening builds all three core weapons before opening wall jobs;
- an incomplete or unverified forecast cannot claim that no safety plan
  exists.

No full night forecast, attack policy, task value, treasure rule, item policy,
or opponent-pressure flag is enabled by this increment.

## Evidence and behavior

The default loadout contains gatling, railgun, and rocket. Each costs 25 gold,
while the economy profile reserves 25 of the initial 75 gold. Before this
change, the ordinary job path could therefore fund only two weapons. The
successful opponent in the supplied match spent its opening 75 gold on three
weapons before starting its wall line.

The director now keeps the 25-gold reserve for every non-defense action but
marks only missing day-one loadout weapons as reserve eligible. Existing job
and joint validation still enforce total gold and prevent items or other
discretionary actions from consuming the reserve.

`minimum_weapons_before_walls` is now three for the default three-weapon
loadout. Job generation counts weapons at their planned sites and clamps the
threshold to the configured loadout length, so shorter custom loadouts do not
deadlock wall construction.

`find_cheapest_safe_plan` now reports `UNKNOWN` when the forecast is
incomplete or a missing weapon has no verified defense gain. `UNAVAILABLE`
is reserved for a complete forecast whose verified action combinations cannot
reach safety. This fixes telemetry meaning; it does not invent wall, upgrade,
or railgun values.

## Limitations

The synthetic replay changes only the round number on repository day/night
fixtures. Its night fixture has far fewer hostile robots than the supplied
35-robot match, and it does not simulate movement, damage, build feedback, or
platform socket latency. It proves bounded code paths and protocol legality,
not that the station will survive round 94.

A real platform R1-R130 replay remains required before further tuning of wall
count, weapon order, task timing, or reserve size. The next comparison should
record weapon completion rounds, wall completion count at R70, station health
through R130, kills, empty-action rounds, request P99/max, and every fallback
reason.
