# v1.4.5 Governor observability and staged fortification

## Scope

This increment addresses two failures found in the September 21 replays:

- night computation could spend its budget without completing a search root,
  poison the governor estimate, and eventually exceed the platform exception
  limit;
- the day planner could wait forever for three weapon types at exact planned
  coordinates, so no wall job was ever generated after a failed or displaced
  weapon build.

## Night reliability

- Governor root-cost EWMA now learns only from completed roots and excludes
  failed Full-attempt time from a later successful Lite sample.
- Phase 3 reports requested, effective, and executed levels, completed roots,
  and a deterministic skip or fallback reason.
- Forecast provenance reports generated round, updated round, age, margin
  source, and whether the value is a conservative bound.
- A missing or destroyed own station clears stale forecasts and skips forecast,
  controller assignment, and search.
- Under critical or lethal risk, a completed Phase 3 result may not replace a
  baseline containing attacks with a zero-attack result. Such a result falls
  back to Phase 2 with `phase3_attack_regression` telemetry.

## Staged fortification

The default daylight order is now:

1. build one usable core weapon;
2. build the three threat-facing critical walls, leaving the planned entrance;
3. complete the gatling, railgun, and rocket loadout;
4. fill the remaining planned wall sites;
5. on every later day, restore critical gaps before ordinary infill.

Weapon readiness is based on living weapon types rather than exact placement
and is shared by Jobs, Features, Director, and Safety. Day two and later may
restore critical walls even when the previous night destroyed every weapon.

Wall candidates are filtered for current occupancy, reachability, and a
one-turn cooldown after a failed wall build before the per-worker candidate
limit is applied. This prevents four bad front candidates from starving every
later gap. Critical wall material demand can override market holding behavior
when a full worker backpack must be cleared before mining stone.

Damaged-but-living wall repair remains disabled until WallFixer behavior is
confirmed by the game protocol. The strategy rebuilds destroyed walls; it does
not yet repair partial wall health.

## Wall telemetry

Each daylight record now includes wall stage, core weapon readiness, planned
and missing walls, missing critical walls, actionable walls, generated wall
jobs, worker stone, stone reserve, last failed wall builds, and a blocker such
as `waiting_for_first_weapon`, `worker_without_stone`,
`failed_build_cooldown`, or `blocked_or_unreachable`.
